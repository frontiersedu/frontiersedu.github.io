The People Behind It All

Meet Our
Team

Our diverse team of professionals with experience spanning management, engineering, education, and travel is united by a single passion: making the world's best learning experiences accessible to every student.

Founder & Director

Vishal Verma

Founder & Director

Vishal founded Frontiers Education driven by a passion for travel and education. He has over 32 years of experience in the Information Technology, Education and Events sectors. He holds a B.E. in Electronics & Telecom from Pune University and an MBA (Finance) from Symbiosis Institute of Business Management, Pune.

He serves as Honorary Director of Global Events for the World Scholar's Cup, a role that reflects Frontiers' exceptional contribution to this global academic programme.

Dipankar Arora

Head - Operations

Dipankar oversees Pan-India operations for the World Scholar's Cup and plays a pivotal role in the planning and execution of global events for Indian school delegations.

He holds a Bachelor of Engineering degree in Electrical and Electronics from Gulbarga University.

With over 30 years of experience spanning both Government and Corporate sectors, he brings extensive operational expertise and strategic leadership to the organisation. His distinguished professional journey includes a significant tenure with the Tata Group of Companies.

Vikram Arora

Vice President - Administration

Vikram oversees Administration at Frontiers Education, bringing over 28 years of professional experience, including a decade-long tenure in the IT industry. A top university ranker, he holds a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering from the Government College of Engineering, Pune.

Driven by a people-first philosophy, Vikram’s positivity has guided the company to new heights of operational excellence, particularly in ensuring a warm, supportive environment for students. Grounded by 30 years of dedicated meditation practice, he actively writes for a global audience on stress management, positive thinking, and emotional intelligence.

Dexter Noakes

General Manager - South India

A commerce graduate from Bangalore University, Dexter brings over 25 years of experience in the travel industry. Based in South India, he leads regional operations and maintains relationships with over a hundred top schools across the region.

Harit Yadav

Accounts & Administration Manager

Harit manages the accounts and administration functions at Frontiers, ensuring every programme runs smoothly behind the scenes. He holds a degree in commerce.

Edwin Joseph

Outreach Manager

Edwin is an Outreach Manager at the World Scholar's Cup. He works with schools across India to expand access to the program. He is passionate about creating enriching academic opportunities for students.

An alumnus of Ashoka University, Edwin has five years of experience as a communications professional.

Ipshita Bagchi

Outreach Coordinator

Ipshita holds an MSc in Industrial and Corporate Psychology from O.P. Jindal Global University. Fascinated by human behaviour and the wonderfully unpredictable ways people think and interact, she is especially interested in what inspires people to step outside their comfort zones and discover new possibilities.

As the Outreach Coordinator for the World Scholar's Cup in India, she works closely with schools and educators to coordinate programmes, foster meaningful partnerships and help create opportunities for students.

Ipshita believes that learning should be challenging, curiosity should be encouraged and that some of the best journeys begin with a simple conversation.
