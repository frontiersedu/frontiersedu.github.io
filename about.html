Who We Are

Global Experiential Learning from India

Frontiers Education offers enrichment programmes from around the world that add a new dimension to learning. Our programmes are designed to supplement the education offered in schools through a variety of practical experiences that go beyond the classroom — experiences that teach, motivate, and inspire children to discover and pursue their passions and be ready to take their place in a globalised world of tomorrow.

As the exclusive Indian partner for the World Scholar's Cup, we have reached 5,000+ students across 200+ schools, running 12 regional rounds each year in cities spanning the length and breadth of India. Each participation is an experience that has the potential to transform a young life.

Our team is diverse and has professionals with vast experience in the fields of management, engineering, education, and travel. We have office locations in Gurgaon — National Capital Region and Chennai, with representatives in other major cities across India.

Our Two Entities

Two Companies, One Purpose

Frontiers operates through two complementary legal entities — each focused on a specific dimension of our work, together covering the full spectrum of educational enrichment and student travel.

🎓

Frontiers Edutainment LLP

Our educational arm curates, partners, and delivers academic enrichment programmes for school students. It manages the intellectual and programmatic side of everything we do — from the World Scholar's Cup to leadership workshops.

This entity holds our exclusive partnership with the World Scholar's Cup as India's representative, manages relationships with global academic institutions, and develops the content and structure of all student programmes.

Academic Competitions Global Partnerships Programme Design School Relations
✈️

Frontiers Education and Travel LLP

Our travel arm handles the logistics, safety, and on-the-ground delivery that turns an exciting programme into a seamless, stress-free experience — from visa documentation and flights to hotel accommodation, local transport, and student safety protocols.

With operations across India, this entity ensures every student arrives safely, is cared for throughout, and returns home enriched — and inspired.

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

Supplement. Inspire. Transform.

To supplement the education offered in schools through practical, real-world experiences that go beyond the classroom — teaching students skills no textbook can. We exist to inspire children to discover and pursue their passions and to be ready to take their place in a globalised world.

Our Vision

A Globalised Generation of Curious Minds.

We envision a generation of Indian students who are globally connected, culturally curious, intellectually confident, and ready to lead. Every student who passes through a Frontiers programme should return home with a new strength, a new friend from another country, and a lifelong love of learning.

Our History

A Journey Built on Passion

Founded by Vishal Verma in 2010, Frontiers grew from a personal belief that travel and education together have the power to change lives. What began as a small operation in Gurgaon has grown into a nationally recognised educational travel company with offices in two cities and partnerships spanning four continents.

Every milestone reflects a deepening commitment to Indian school students — each partnership forged, each programme launched, each student who walked away transformed is part of our story.

Meet the Founders Get In Touch
2016

Frontiers Education and Travel LLP Formed

A dedicated travel entity is created to professionally manage the logistics, safety, and on-ground operations behind every programme.

2017

South India Office Opens — Chennai

Expanding reach across India, a South India office opens under General Manager Dexter Noakes, bringing Frontiers programmes to schools across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Kerala.

2019

WSC Honorary Directorship

Founder Vishal Verma is appointed Honorary Director of Global Events for the World Scholar's Cup — recognising Frontiers' exceptional contribution to the global programme.

2024

5,000+ Students & Growing

With 200+ school partnerships, 12 regional rounds annually, 5,000+ students reached, and offices in two cities, Frontiers continues to grow — guided by the same founding passion for transformative education.
