Who We Are

Where the Classroom Meets the World

Founded in 2010, Frontiers Education is the exclusive partner for the World Scholar's Cup in India, working with 200+ schools, organizing 12 regional rounds, and reaching 5,000+ students across India each year.

Our diverse team, with experience spanning event management, education and travel, makes every detail effortless, from visas and flights to on-the-ground arrangements.
