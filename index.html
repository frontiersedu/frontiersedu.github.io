Featured Programme

The World Scholar's Cup

The World Scholar's Cup is an annual international academic tournament and enrichment program that features essay writing, debates, and quizzes to address real world scenarios. Each year over 50,000 students on six continents participate in the program.

Collaborations

Space University @ Space Center Houston

Spend five days at NASA's Johnson Space Center, working alongside expert educators on space science, robotics, and engineering challenges inspired by real missions. Through project-based learning, exclusive facility access, and interactions with NASA scientists and astronauts, students gain a deeper understanding of innovation and exploration.

Space Camp @ U.S. Space & Rocket Center

Train like an astronaut through mission simulations, rocket launches, advanced training devices, and hands-on STEM activities at the U.S. Space & Rocket Center. Designed for students aged 9–18, the programme develops leadership, teamwork, and problem-solving skills in a dynamic and highly interactive environment.

Camp Kennedy Space Center

Explore the home of America's most iconic space missions through exclusive tours, astronaut experiences, and engineering design challenges at NASA's Kennedy Space Center. From launch pads and mission control facilities to the Space Shuttle Atlantis, students discover the history, technology, and future of human spaceflight.

Euro Space Camp

Explore the frontiers of science, space, and discovery through a ten-day immersive programme spanning Belgium, France, and Switzerland. From astronaut training and rocket building to a behind-the-scenes visit to CERN, students gain unforgettable STEM experiences while exploring some of Europe's most iconic destinations.

Foróige Leadership for Life

Develop the mindset and skills of an effective leader at Ireland's renowned Foróige International Youth Leadership Conference. Endorsed by UNESCO, this immersive programme combines leadership training, global collaboration, and personal development to inspire the next generation of changemakers.
